  1. Drop your WebP images into the box, or click to browse your files.

Why WebP images cause problems

WebP was designed for fast websites, and browsers show it happily. The trouble starts when you save such a picture and try to use it elsewhere: some editors, social uploads, print services and older versions of office software refuse it. Converting it to JPG solves the problem for photographs.

Transparent WebP files

Many WebP graphics have transparent backgrounds. JPG fills those areas with white, so for logos and cut-outs choose PNG from the format menu instead.

Animated WebP

Animated WebP files are converted to a still image of the first frame, because JPG cannot hold animation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert WebP to JPG?

Add the WebP file, keep JPG selected as the output, and download the converted picture.

Why can’t I open WebP files?

Some older programs do not support WebP. Converting to JPG or PNG makes the image compatible.

Does WebP to JPG lose quality?

Slightly, because JPG is lossy. At 90% quality the difference is hard to see.

What about transparency?

JPG cannot be transparent. Choose PNG if you need it.

Will animated WebP stay animated?

No. The output is a still image of the first frame.

Can I convert several files?

Yes. Batch conversion is supported, with a Download all button.

Is it private?

Yes. The files are processed in your browser and never uploaded.
