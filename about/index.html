About CalcNest

CalcNest is a growing library of free calculators that take the guesswork out of home projects, hobbies, pet care and everyday decisions.

Why these calculators exist

Anyone who has poured too little epoxy, run out of yarn one row from the end of a blanket, or bought three cans of paint too many knows how frustrating a bad estimate is. Most online calculators are cluttered, hide their maths, or simply give an answer without explaining it.

CalcNest was created to fix that. Every tool is designed to be fast on a phone, clear about its assumptions, and open about its formula, so you can check the numbers and adjust them for your own project.

What you can expect

  • Transparent formulas. Each page shows the exact equation and a worked example.
  • Private by design. Calculations run in your browser; the numbers you enter are never sent to our servers.
  • Imperial and metric. Made for readers in the US, UK, Canada and Australia.
  • Honest limits. Results are estimates, and we say when a product label or a professional should have the final word.
