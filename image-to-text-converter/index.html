Image to Text Converter

Upload a picture of text — a screenshot, scan or photo of a page — and get editable text you can copy or download. Your image stays on your device.

How to use this tool

  1. Drop an image into the box, click to choose one, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V.
  2. Select the language of the text for the most accurate result.
  3. Press Extract text and wait while the progress bar completes. The first run downloads the language data.
  4. Edit the text if needed, then copy it or download it as a .txt file.

How OCR works

1. clean the image and find lines of text 2. split lines into words and characters 3. a trained model predicts each character 4. the characters are joined into text // runs in your browser using Tesseract.js (open source)
Under the hoodA sharp, well-lit screenshot of an English paragraph usually comes out with 95% or higher accuracy. A blurry, tilted or low-contrast photo can drop well below that — use the tips in the table below.

Tips for accurate text recognition

Do thisWhy it helps
Use a sharp, high-resolution pictureLetters need enough pixels to be recognised
Keep the page straight and flatTilted or curved text confuses line detection
Make sure the lighting is evenShadows reduce contrast between text and paper
Choose the correct languageThe model uses language-specific patterns
Crop out pictures and bordersNon-text areas produce random characters

Handwriting and decorative fonts are much harder for OCR and may need manual correction.

What you can use OCR for

OCR (optical character recognition) turns text in a picture into text you can search, edit and copy. It is handy for digitising printed pages, pulling quotes from a screenshot, copying text from a whiteboard photo, reading old documents, or extracting details from receipts and business cards.

Privacy

Your image is processed locally in your browser and is never uploaded to a server. The tool downloads the open-source recognition engine and the language data the first time you use it, which is why the first run takes a little longer.

Supported languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Arabic, Urdu and Hindi. Pick the one that matches your text; mixed-language documents work best when you choose the main language.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an image to text?

Add the image, choose the language, press Extract text and copy the result when it appears.

Is this image to text converter free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up, and there is no limit on the number of images.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The recognition runs in your browser. Only the OCR engine and language data are downloaded.

Why is the text inaccurate?

Blurry, small, tilted or low-contrast images reduce accuracy. Try a clearer picture and make sure the correct language is selected.

Can it read handwriting?

Not reliably. It is designed for printed text, and handwriting usually needs manual correction.

Which image formats work?

JPG, PNG, WebP and BMP. For PDFs, take a screenshot of the page first.

Why does the first conversion take longer?

The engine and language data download the first time, then your browser can reuse them.
