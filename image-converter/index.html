Image Converter

Drop in one or more pictures, choose the format you need, and download the converted files. Nothing is uploaded — it all happens in your browser.

How to use this tool

  1. Drag your images onto the box, or click it to choose files. You can add several at once.
  2. Pick the format to convert to: PNG, JPG or WebP.
  3. For JPG and WebP, set the quality. Around 85–90% is a good balance of size and sharpness.
  4. Press Download beside a file, or Download all to save everything.

How the conversion works

1. your browser decodes the image into raw pixels 2. the pixels are drawn on an off-screen canvas 3. the canvas is re-encoded as PNG, JPG or WebP 4. the new file is handed to you as a download // no upload, no server, no account
In plain wordsConverting a 3 MB PNG photo to JPG at 85% quality typically gives a file a fraction of the size with no visible difference. Converting the same picture to WebP is often smaller still. Transparent areas turn white when you save as JPG, because JPG cannot store transparency.

JPG and WebP are “lossy”: lower quality means smaller files. PNG is lossless, so the quality slider does not apply.

Which format should you choose?

Use JPG for photographs and anything shared widely, since every device opens it. Choose PNG when you need sharp edges, text or transparency, such as logos and screenshots. WebP gives smaller files than both and is supported by all current browsers, which makes it ideal for websites that need to load quickly — but a few older programs and some upload forms still reject it.

Why the file size changes

Different formats store the same picture in different ways. Converting a photo from PNG to JPG throws away detail that the eye barely notices, so the file shrinks a lot. Going the other way — JPG to PNG — cannot restore detail that is already gone, and the PNG will usually be larger, not better.

Your files stay on your device

Many converters upload pictures to a server. This one does not: the work is done by your own browser, so private photos, ID scans and client images never leave your computer or phone. It also means there is no file-size queue, and it keeps working even on a slow connection once the page has loaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change an image file extension?

Renaming the extension does not convert a file. Use a converter: add the image, choose the new format and download the result, which is properly re-encoded.

Is it safe to convert private photos here?

Yes. The conversion happens inside your browser and the image is never sent to our servers.

Will converting reduce the quality?

Converting to PNG is lossless. JPG and WebP are lossy, but at 85–90% quality the difference is rarely visible.

Why does my transparent PNG get a white background?

JPG does not support transparency, so transparent areas are filled with white. Choose PNG or WebP to keep transparency.

Can I convert many images at once?

Yes. Add several files together; each is converted and listed with its own download button.

Does it support HEIC from iPhones?

Most browsers cannot open HEIC. Safari can. Otherwise, set your iPhone camera to “Most Compatible” or share the photo as JPG.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no upload limit, but very large images need a lot of memory. If a huge file fails, try resizing it first.
