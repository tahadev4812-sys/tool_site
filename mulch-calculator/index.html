How to use this calculator

  1. Choose imperial or metric and measure the length and width of the bed. Split curved beds into simple rectangles.
  2. Enter the depth of mulch you want to spread.
  3. Keep the 5% extra for settling, or raise it for uneven ground.
  4. Press Calculate to see bulk volume and the number of bags.

The formula

volume (ft³) = length × width × depth (in) ÷ 12 cubic yards = ft³ ÷ 27 · cubic meters = ft³ ÷ 35.315 bags = ft³ ÷ bag size (2 or 3 ft³)
Worked exampleA 20 × 10 ft bed with 3 in of mulch: 20 × 10 × 3 ÷ 12 = 50 ft³. Add 5% = 52.5 ft³ = 1.94 cubic yards, or 27 bags of 2 cu ft.

How deep should mulch be?

Two to three inches suits most flower and shrub beds: enough to block weeds and hold moisture without smothering roots. Use 3–4 inches around trees and in play areas, where wood chips also cushion falls. A deeper layer is not better — mulch piled against stems traps moisture and invites pests and rot.

Bags or bulk?

Bagged mulch is convenient for small beds, but each 2 cu ft bag covers only 12 sq ft at 2 inches deep. A cubic yard is the same as 13.5 two-cubic-foot bags and is usually much cheaper when delivered by the truck-load. If you need more than two yards, ask local landscape suppliers for a quote.

Choosing a mulch

  • Bark and wood chips: long-lasting, good for trees and paths.
  • Shredded hardwood: knits together on slopes.
  • Compost or leaf mould: feeds soil in vegetable beds.
  • Gravel: permanent, but does not add nutrients.

Frequently asked questions

How much mulch do I need for a 10 × 20 bed?

At 3 inches deep, a 200 sq ft bed needs 50 cubic feet, or about 1.85 cubic yards, plus a little extra for settling.

How many bags of mulch are in a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet, so it takes 13.5 bags of 2 cu ft or 9 bags of 3 cu ft.

How deep should I spread mulch?

Usually 2–4 inches. Thinner layers do not suppress weeds; thicker layers can suffocate roots.

How often should I replace mulch?

Top up annually. Organic mulch decomposes, so refresh the layer to its original depth each year or two.

Do I need landscape fabric under mulch?

Not always. Fabric can help on paths, but in planting beds it often stops organic mulch from feeding the soil.

Can I use the calculator for gravel?

Yes — the volume is calculated the same way. For weight, multiply the cubic yards by the density from your supplier.

What does the metric option give me?

Cubic meters, liters and bags of 50 liters, for UK, Canadian and Australian suppliers.
