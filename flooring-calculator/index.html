How to use this calculator

  1. Choose feet or meters and enter the room’s length and width. For L-shaped rooms, calculate each part and add the boxes.
  2. Enter the coverage per box from the packaging.
  3. Choose a waste allowance for your layout — more for diagonal or patterned floors.
  4. Optionally enter the price per box to see the total cost.
  5. Press Calculate and buy the number of boxes shown.

The formula

area = length × width needed = area × (1 + waste% ÷ 100) boxes = ceil( needed ÷ coverage per box )
Worked exampleA 15 × 12 ft room has 180 sq ft. With 10% waste you need 198 sq ft. At 20 sq ft per box: 198 ÷ 20 = 9.9, so buy 10 boxes (200 sq ft), leaving 20 sq ft spare.

How much waste should you allow?

Waste covers cut-offs at walls, doorways and corners, plus the odd damaged plank. For a straight lay in a simple rectangle, 5–10% is typical. Diagonal installs need more because every board is cut at an angle, and herringbone or chevron patterns can use 15–20%. Rooms with many alcoves, stairs or angled walls also need extra.

Before you buy

  • Measure at the widest and longest points, and add closets and door openings you will floor.
  • Let planks acclimatise in the room for the time on the box, usually 48 hours.
  • Check subfloor flatness — most floating floors need it flat within 3/16 in over 10 ft.
  • Buy all boxes in one go so the batch numbers match.

Underlay and trim

This calculator covers the flooring itself. Underlay is sold by area, so use the room area plus a few percent, and trim such as skirting, thresholds and quarter-round is sold by length — measure the perimeter.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate how much flooring I need?

Multiply room length by width for the area, add 5–15% for waste, and divide by the coverage on each box, rounding up.

How much extra flooring should I buy?

10% is standard for straight installs. Use 15% or more for diagonal layouts and 20% for herringbone.

Do I measure under cabinets and appliances?

No, you only need to floor what is visible, though some installers floor under dishwashers to ease replacement.

How many square feet are in a box of laminate?

It varies by brand. Many laminate and vinyl plank boxes cover 18–24 sq ft; check the label.

Does this work for tile?

Yes, enter the coverage per carton and use 10–15% waste. Large-format and diagonal tile needs the upper end.

Can I use metric?

Yes. Switch to metric and enter meters and square meters of coverage per pack.

Why keep spare boxes?

Repairs later are much easier when you have planks from the same batch.
