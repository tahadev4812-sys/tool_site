Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information CalcNest (“we”, “us”, “calcnest.com”) collects when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information you enter in calculators

All calculations run locally in your browser. The numbers you type into our calculators are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

Information we collect automatically

Like most websites, we and our partners may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, time spent and referring website. This data is used to operate, secure and improve the site.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and third parties use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic and show advertising. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as well.

Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect information such as pages viewed and approximate location, and sends it to Google. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We may display ads served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies by visiting aboutads.info. Visitors in the UK and EU can also use youronlinechoices.com. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Affiliate links

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Your rights

To make a request, email hello@calcnest.com.

Children’s privacy

CalcNest is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with the date above updated.

Contact
