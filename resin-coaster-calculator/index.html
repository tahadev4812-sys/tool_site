Resin Coaster Calculator

Planning a batch of coasters? Enter the size, thickness and how many you are pouring to see exactly how much resin and hardener to mix.

How to use this calculator

  1. Choose round or square and your measuring unit.
  2. Enter the diameter (round) or side length (square) of the mold cavity, then the resin thickness.
  3. Enter how many coasters you will pour at once.
  4. Keep the extra at 15% and press Calculate.
  5. Mix the shown resin and hardener amounts together, then fill every cavity.

The formula

round: volume = π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × thickness square: volume = side × side × thickness total = volume × coasters × (1 + waste% ÷ 100) fluid ounces = in³ ÷ 1.805 · ml = in³ ÷ 61.024 × 1000
Worked exampleFour round 4 in coasters, 1/4 in thick: π × 2² × 0.25 = 3.14 in³ each, so 12.57 in³ for the batch. Add 15% = 14.45 in³ ≈ 8.0 fl oz (237 ml) — about 4 fl oz resin and 4 fl oz hardener at 1:1.

Why coasters need a bigger safety margin

The smaller the pour, the larger the share of resin that clings to the cup, stir stick and spreader. That is why this calculator starts at 15% extra instead of 10%. If you use pigments, glitter or inclusions, they displace a little volume but you should still mix the full amount so every coaster is the same colour.

Thickness and heat

Most coasters are 1/4 to 3/8 inch thick, which is well within the pour limit for tabletop epoxy. If you embed flowers, shells or geodes, measure the mold cavity depth rather than the item height, and seal porous inclusions first so they do not release air bubbles into the resin.

Finishing tips

  • Pop bubbles with a heat gun or torch held a hand’s width above the surface.
  • Cover the molds to keep dust out while the resin cures.
  • Cure fully before using them with hot or wet cups, and add cork or felt feet to protect furniture.

Frequently asked questions

How much resin do I need for one 4 inch coaster?

A round 4 in coaster poured 1/4 in thick holds about 3.1 cubic inches, which is roughly 1.7 fl oz of mixed epoxy before waste.

How thick should resin coasters be?

Between 1/4 and 3/8 inch is typical. Thinner coasters can feel fragile; thicker ones use more resin.

How much extra resin should I mix for a coaster batch?

15% is a sensible allowance because a high proportion of resin is left behind in cups and on tools.

Can I use the same resin as for a table?

Check the product label. Many tabletop epoxies work well for coasters, but heat resistance varies, so look for a brand that lists a heat rating.

Do I need to measure the mold?

Yes. Measure the cavity itself, since the outside dimensions of silicone molds are larger.

What if my coasters are hexagons or irregular shapes?

Fill one cavity with water, pour it into a measuring cup and multiply that volume by the number of coasters.

Does the calculator support metric?

Yes. Switch to centimeters and the result is still shown in fluid ounces and milliliters.
