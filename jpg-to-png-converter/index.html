JPG to PNG Converter

Add your JPG or JPEG pictures and get PNG files back in a second. Your images never leave your device.

How to use this tool

  1. Drop your JPG or JPEG files into the box, or click to browse.
  2. Leave Convert to on PNG — it is already selected for this tool.
  3. Wait a moment for each file to appear in the list with its new size.
  4. Click Download for one file, or Download all for the whole batch.

What happens to your JPG

JPG → decoded to pixels → PNG (lossless) PNG keeps every pixel exactly as the JPG shows it but cannot bring back detail JPG already discarded
Good to knowA 1.2 MB JPG photo often becomes a 4–8 MB PNG. The picture looks identical, because PNG stores the image without further loss — it just uses far more space than JPG’s compressed format.

Why people convert JPG to PNG

JPG saves space by discarding detail every time it is compressed, so re-saving a JPG repeatedly makes it worse. Converting to PNG once locks in the current quality, which is useful before editing, adding text or layering a picture in a design. PNG is also required by some design tools, app stores and templates.

Expect a bigger file

PNG compresses without loss, so photographs make large files. That is normal. If size matters more than perfect fidelity — for a website, for example — keep the JPG or convert it to WebP instead.

Private by design

Conversion happens locally in your browser. Your pictures are not uploaded, stored or shared, which makes this safe for personal and work images.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a JPG to PNG?

Add the JPG file to the box above, leave the format on PNG, and download the converted file when it appears.

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. It keeps the quality you already have, but cannot restore detail lost when the JPG was created.

Why is my PNG much larger than the JPG?

PNG stores the image without lossy compression, which takes more space for photographs.

Will the PNG have a transparent background?

No. A JPG has no transparency, so the PNG will keep the same solid background. Remove it with an image editor if you need transparency.

Can I convert several JPG files at once?

Yes, add as many as you like and use Download all.

Are JPG and JPEG the same?

Yes. They are the same format; “jpg” is the three-letter version of the extension.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
