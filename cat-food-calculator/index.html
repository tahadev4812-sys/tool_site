Cat Food Calculator

Enter your cat’s weight and life stage to estimate daily calories, then turn that into cups or cans using the calorie count on your cat’s food.

kcal
See the label. Dry food is often 300–450 kcal per cup; a 3 oz can is often 70–100 kcal.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your cat’s weight in pounds or kilograms.
  2. Pick the life stage or goal that fits best.
  3. Find the calories per cup or can on your cat food’s packaging and enter it.
  4. Choose the number of meals and press Calculate.
  5. Weigh your cat monthly and adjust the portion if their weight changes.

The formula

RER (kcal/day) = 70 × (weight in kg)^0.75 daily kcal = RER × life stage factor factors: neutered adult 1.2 · intact adult 1.4 · kitten 2.5 inactive 1.0 · weight loss 0.8 portion = daily kcal ÷ kcal per cup (or can)
Worked exampleA neutered adult cat of 10 lb (4.54 kg): RER = 70 × 4.54^0.75 ≈ 218 kcal. × 1.2 = 261 kcal per day. With 350 kcal per cup of dry food, that is 0.75 cup per day, or about 3/8 cup at each of two meals.

Calories matter more than cups

The same cup can hold very different amounts of energy depending on the food, so the calories on the packaging are what count. A cup of one dry food may have 300 kcal and another 450. That is why the calculator asks for the food’s calorie content instead of guessing. Use a kitchen scale if you can: weighing food is much more accurate than scooping.

Wet food, dry food and mixing

Wet food has more water and fewer calories per ounce, and it helps cats get extra moisture. If you feed both, split the daily calories between them — for example half from wet and half from dry — and work out each portion separately from its own calorie count.

Signs your cat’s portion is right

  • You can feel but not see the ribs.
  • A visible waist when viewed from above.
  • A steady weight over several weeks.

Treats should make up no more than about 10% of daily calories.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I feed my cat per day?

An average neutered 10 lb adult cat needs roughly 260 calories per day. The right portion depends on the calories in your cat’s food, and on age, activity and health.

How many calories does a kitten need?

Kittens need about 2.5 times their resting energy requirement because they are growing fast, so they eat more per pound than adult cats.

How do I calculate cat food portions?

Work out daily calories, then divide by the calories in one cup or can of your food. The calculator does this and splits the total over your chosen number of meals.

Should I feed my cat wet or dry food?

Both can provide complete nutrition. Wet food adds moisture; dry food is convenient and stays fresh longer. Ask your vet if your cat has a health condition.

My cat is overweight. What do I do?

Choose “weight loss”, but speak with your vet first. Crash diets are dangerous for cats because fast weight loss can cause liver disease.

How often should I feed my cat?

Two meals a day suits most adult cats. Kittens usually need three or four smaller meals.

Is the calculator a replacement for veterinary advice?

No. It gives a standard estimate only; your cat’s health and your vet’s advice always take priority.
