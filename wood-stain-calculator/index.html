How to use this calculator

  1. Choose your unit and enter the length and width of the surface.
  2. Choose whether you will stain one side or both sides, and how many coats.
  3. Enter the coverage printed on the can. If you are not sure, keep the default.
  4. Press Calculate and buy the amount shown, rounded up.

The formula

area = length × width × sides stain (gal) = area × coats ÷ coverage per gallon liters = gallons × 3.785 · quarts = gallons × 4
Worked exampleA 16 × 12 ft deck, one side, one coat: 192 sq ft. At 300 sq ft per gallon, that is 0.64 gallons (about 2.6 quarts), so one gallon can covers it with some left for touch-ups.

What changes how far stain goes

Stain is absorbed rather than sitting on top of the wood, so the species and condition of the surface matter. Soft, porous woods such as pine and cedar drink more than dense hardwoods, and bare, sun-weathered boards absorb more than new, smooth ones. Semi-transparent stains are applied thinly; solid stains behave more like paint and may cover less area per gallon.

Preparing the surface

  • Clean off dirt, mildew and old finish; let the wood dry fully.
  • Sand to the grit recommended on the can.
  • Test the stain on a hidden area to check the colour.
  • Apply with the grain and wipe off any excess within the time on the label.

One coat or two?

Many penetrating stains need only one coat; a second coat darkens the colour but is not always absorbed. Always follow the product’s instructions, because over-applied stain can stay tacky.

Frequently asked questions

How much stain do I need for a 12 × 16 deck?

A 192 sq ft deck needs roughly 0.64 gallons for one coat at 300 sq ft per gallon, so one gallon is enough.

How many square feet does a gallon of stain cover?

Usually 200–400 sq ft depending on the product and wood. Read the can for the exact rate.

Should I stain both sides of a fence?

Staining both sides protects the boards better and helps prevent uneven weathering. Choose “both sides” in the calculator to double the area.

Do I need two coats of wood stain?

Not always. Follow the label — many stains are designed for a single coat.

Can I use the calculator for furniture?

Yes. Enter the length and width of each surface and add them together, or calculate one piece at a time.

Does this include sealer or varnish?

No. Calculate the topcoat separately using its own coverage rate.

Does it work in metric?

Yes. Switch to meters and give the coverage in m² per liter.
