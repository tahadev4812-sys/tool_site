Dog Food Calculator

Enter your dog’s weight and life stage to estimate daily calories, then turn that into cups or grams using the calorie count on your dog’s food.

lb
For weight loss, enter the ideal (target) weight.

kcal
See the label. Dry dog food is often 300–450 kcal per cup.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your dog’s weight in pounds or kilograms.
  2. Pick the life stage or goal that matches your dog.
  3. Enter the calories per cup from the food’s packaging.
  4. Choose the meals per day and press Calculate.
  5. Check your dog’s weight and body shape every few weeks, and adjust the portion by about 10% if needed.

The formula

RER (kcal/day) = 70 × (weight in kg)^0.75 daily kcal = RER × life stage factor factors: neutered adult 1.6 · intact adult 1.8 · inactive 1.4 weight loss 1.0 · puppy <4 months 3.0 · puppy 4+ months 2.0 active / working 3.0 cups per day = daily kcal ÷ kcal per cup
Worked exampleA neutered adult dog weighing 40 lb (18.1 kg): RER = 70 × 18.1^0.75 ≈ 616 kcal. × 1.6 = 985 kcal per day. At 370 kcal per cup, that is 2.7 cups per day, or 1.3 cups at each of two meals.

Why calories beat the feeding chart on the bag

Feeding charts on pet food bags are broad averages and tend to overestimate for neutered, indoor dogs. Working from your dog’s own energy requirement gives a far more tailored target. The resting energy figure estimates what your dog burns just to stay alive, and the life-stage factor adds the energy used for growth, activity and recovery.

Check body condition, not just numbers

Use the result as a starting point, then watch your dog. You should be able to feel the ribs under a thin layer of fat, see a waist from above and a tucked belly from the side. If your dog is gaining, cut the portion by 10%; if they look too thin, add 10% and talk to your vet.

Treats and extras

Treats, chews and table scraps add up quickly. A good rule is to keep them under 10% of the daily calories and reduce the main meal to match. Training treats can be broken into pea-sized pieces.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I feed my dog per day?

It depends on weight, age and activity. A neutered 40 lb adult dog needs roughly 985 calories per day, about 2.7 cups of a 370 kcal-per-cup food.

How many times a day should I feed a dog?

Two meals a day suit most adult dogs. Puppies often need three or four smaller meals.

How do I find the calories in my dog food?

Look for “kcal per cup” or “metabolizable energy” on the packaging or the manufacturer’s website.

How much should a puppy eat?

Puppies need much more energy per pound than adults — roughly two to three times resting energy. Follow your vet’s guidance and adjust as they grow.

My dog is overweight. What should I do?

Select “weight loss” and enter the target weight. Slow, steady loss under a vet’s supervision is safest.

Is a cup the same as 100 grams?

Roughly for dry kibble, but kibble density varies a lot, so weigh the food on a scale for accuracy.

Is this a substitute for vet advice?

No. It is a general guide; dogs with health conditions have special needs.
