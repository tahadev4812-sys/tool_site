How to use this calculator

  1. Choose imperial or metric units and enter the total area to be tiled.
  2. Enter the tile length and width. For square tiles, enter the same number twice.
  3. Enter the tile thickness (which sets joint depth) and the grout joint width.
  4. Keep the 10% waste allowance for mixing losses and spills, and press Calculate.
  5. Buy the number of bags shown. Leftover grout is useful for later repairs.

The formula

joint volume (in³) = area (in²) × (tile L + tile W) ÷ (tile L × tile W) × joint width × joint depth grout weight (lb) = joint volume × 0.0672 // cement grout ≈ 1.86 kg/L total = weight × (1 + waste% ÷ 100)
Worked example100 sq ft of 12 × 12 in tile with 1/8 in joints, 3/8 in deep: area = 14,400 in². (12 + 12) ÷ 144 = 0.1667. Volume = 14,400 × 0.1667 × 0.125 × 0.375 = 112.5 in³ → 7.56 lb. Add 10% waste = 8.3 lb, so one 10 lb bag is enough.

Why tile size changes grout so much

Grout fills the gaps between tiles, so what matters is the total length of the joints. A square foot of 4-inch tile has three times as much joint as a square foot of 12-inch tile. Wider and deeper joints add still more volume. That is why mosaic and subway tile projects need many bags of grout while large-format porcelain needs very little.

Choosing joint width

Joint width is set by the tile type. Rectified porcelain is often laid with narrow 1/16 to 1/8 inch joints; hand-made or natural-stone tiles need 3/16 inch or wider to hide variations in size. Unsanded grout suits joints up to 1/8 inch; sanded grout resists shrinking and cracking in wider joints.

Mixing and applying grout

  • Mix only what you can use in 30 minutes.
  • Pack the joints with a rubber float held at 45°, then remove excess diagonally.
  • Wipe with a damp sponge once the grout has firmed up, and seal cement-based grout after it has cured.

Frequently asked questions

How much grout do I need for 100 square feet of tile?

It depends on tile size. With 12 × 12 in tile and 1/8 in joints, about 8 lb including waste. With 4 × 4 in tile it is around 25 lb.

How do I calculate grout for tile?

Multiply the area by the joint length per unit of area, then by joint width and depth to get volume, then by grout density. The formula is shown above and the calculator does it for you.

What should the joint depth be?

Normally the full thickness of the tile — typically 3/8 inch for floor tile and 1/4 inch for wall tile. Enter the tile thickness as the depth.

Sanded or unsanded grout?

Use sanded grout for joints wider than 1/8 inch and unsanded for narrow joints or delicate surfaces that could scratch.

Does the calculator work for metric tile?

Yes. Switch to metric, then enter the area in m² and tile and joint sizes in millimeters.

How much extra grout should I buy?

Ten percent extra is sensible. Uneven substrates, hand-made tile and wide joints can use more.

Does epoxy grout use the same amount?

Roughly, yes, though epoxy grout is sold in kits by volume. Use the liters figure in the result to compare.
