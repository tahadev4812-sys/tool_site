How to use this calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount before the tip.
  2. Type your tip percentage — see the guide below if you are unsure.
  3. Enter how many people are splitting the check.
  4. Choose whether to round shares up to the next whole number, then press Calculate tip.

The formula

tip = bill × tip% ÷ 100 total = bill + tip each person = total ÷ number of people
Worked exampleA bill of 85.50 with an 18% tip: tip = 85.50 × 0.18 = 15.39. Total = 100.89. Split between 4 people, each pays 25.22.

Tip on the pre-tax amount?

Many people tip on the pre-tax subtotal, while others tip on the total. Either is acceptable; the difference on a typical meal is small. Enter whichever amount you prefer as the bill. If your check already includes an automatic service charge, which is common for large parties, you generally do not need to add another tip.

Splitting fairly

An even split is the quickest, but it can feel unfair when some people order much more. In that case, work out each person’s own bill and tip, or round up shares so the server is covered. The “round up” option makes the maths painless — the extra goes to the tip.

Tipping beyond restaurants

  • Delivery drivers and rideshare: 10–20%.
  • Hairdressers and barbers: 10–20%.
  • Taxi: round up or 10%.
  • Hotel housekeeping and porters: a small fixed amount per day or bag.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a 20% tip?

Multiply the bill by 0.2. A quick way is to find 10% by moving the decimal point one place, and then double it.

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

In the US and Canada 15–20% is standard. In the UK 10–12.5% is common if no service charge is included, and in Australia a tip is optional.

Should I tip before or after tax?

Either is acceptable. Many people tip on the pre-tax amount; others use the total.

How do I split a bill with a tip?

Add the tip to the bill and divide the total by the number of people. The calculator shows each person’s share.

What if service was poor?

Many diners lower the tip for genuinely poor service, but first consider whether the problem was in the kitchen or the staff’s control.

Does it work with other currencies?

Yes. Amounts have no currency symbol, so use any currency.

Is a service charge the same as a tip?

Not always. Ask whether it goes to the staff, and do not tip twice unless you want to.
