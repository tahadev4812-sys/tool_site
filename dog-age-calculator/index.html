Dog Age Calculator

Find out how old your dog is in human years. Enter their age and size to compare the classic 7× rule, a size-adjusted chart and the newer scientific formula.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter your dog’s age in years and choose any extra months.
  2. Select the size category that matches your dog’s adult weight.
  3. Press Calculate to see the human-age equivalent from three methods.
  4. Compare the results — the size-adjusted figure is the most practical for everyday use.

The formula

classic = dog age × 7 size-adjusted: year 1 = 15 · year 2 = +9 (total 24) then + 4 (small) · 5 (medium) · 6 (large) · 7 (giant) per year epigenetic (2019) = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31
Worked exampleA 5-year-old medium dog: classic = 35. Size-adjusted = 24 + (5 − 2) × 5 = 39 years. Epigenetic = 16 × ln(5) + 31 = 16 × 1.609 + 31 = 56.8 years.

Why “one dog year equals seven human years” is wrong

The 7× rule is a convenient myth. Dogs mature much faster than people in the first two years — a one-year-old dog is roughly like a 15-year-old teenager — and slow down afterwards. The pace also depends on size: large dogs age faster than small dogs, so a 10-year-old Great Dane is considerably older than a 10-year-old Chihuahua.

The newer epigenetic formula

In 2019, researchers at the University of California, San Diego compared DNA methylation patterns in Labrador Retrievers with those of humans. They found that dogs age rapidly at first and then more slowly, which fits a logarithmic curve: human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31. The study looked at one breed, so it is best viewed as an interesting data point rather than an exact conversion.

Caring for your dog at every life stage

  • Puppies: vaccinations, socialisation and training.
  • Adults: annual check-ups, dental care and a healthy weight.
  • Seniors: twice-yearly vet visits, joint support and softer bedding.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my dog’s age in human years?

The simplest way is to multiply by 7, but a more accurate approach counts the first year as 15 and the second as 9, then adds 4–7 years per year depending on size.

Do big dogs age faster than small dogs?

Yes. Large and giant breeds reach old age earlier and have shorter lifespans than small breeds.

How old is a 1-year-old dog in human years?

About 15 human years. Dogs reach adolescence and sexual maturity within their first year.

What is the epigenetic dog age formula?

It is human age = 16 × ln(dog age) + 31, from a 2019 study of DNA methylation in Labradors.

When is a dog considered a senior?

Small dogs from about 10 years, medium dogs from 8, large dogs from 7 and giant breeds from about 6.

How old is my dog if I adopted it?

Ask your vet to estimate by checking teeth, coat and eyes. Enter that estimate for a rough human age.

Is this a medical tool?

No. It is an estimate for interest and planning only; talk to your vet about your dog’s health.
